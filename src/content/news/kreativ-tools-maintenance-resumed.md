Kreativ Tools is an active Made by KREATIV project again, maintained by Andrei Olaru. The public source repository remains available and contributions are welcome under its published license.

This supersedes the Kreativ Tools maintenance status described in the August contributor-handoff announcement. That announcement remains online as a historical record.

Kreativ Auto is currently paused. Findsera and News Ledger remain open-source contributor projects without an owner maintenance commitment. Made by KREATIV continues its software development, QA services and independent product work.

See the [current Kreativ Tools profile](/projects/kreativ-tools/) or the [project directory](/projects/) for current status and source links.
