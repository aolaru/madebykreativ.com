News Ledger is included in the Made by Kreativ archive as a compact RSS-based briefing demo and reusable Cloudflare Worker reference.

The project page keeps News Ledger discoverable while the <a class="cta" href="https://github.com/aolaru/signal-ledger" target="_blank" rel="noopener">public source repository</a> preserves the implementation for reuse. Open the <a class="cta" href="/projects/signal-ledger/">News Ledger overview</a> for its current status and maintenance scope.

The standalone product is not operated as an active media business. The source remains public as an MIT-licensed portfolio demo without a newsletter, advertising, or monetization roadmap.

The archive includes RSS ingestion, topic filtering, compact article cards, trust pages, and a local development wrapper.
