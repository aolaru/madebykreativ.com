The KREATIV Product-to-Profit guide now uses the free Product Idea Scorecard as its recommended entry point. It helps compare up to three plausible ideas and finish with one evidence-backed Product Brief.

The connected core path remains clear: Idea Shortlist to Product Brief to Product Specification to Launch Record to Product Change Request. The Idea Library and Scorecard are free / pay what you want, Workflow Product Builder and Digital Product Launch Engine are €19 each, and the complete system is €49.

KREATIV AI Business Pathways is also available for €19 as an optional exploration branch when the business model itself needs a focused seven-day evidence experiment. It is not a required first stage.
