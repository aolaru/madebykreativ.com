The October 4 release refreshes all six Product-to-Profit packages. The complete system is **€39**, and the two entry products remain **free / pay what you want** on Gumroad.

Every guide has a text-only HTML reading edition. These are reading alternatives, not interactive form replacements. Editable documents, trackers, and PDF forms remain available in their original formats.

The Builder and complete system both include the fictional case study and six editable sample DOCX files. The bundle adds the integrated workspace and implementation tracks; shared sample files are not presented as exclusive bonuses.

Prices are in EUR before any applicable checkout tax. These tools help organize decisions and work; they do not guarantee customers, sales, or income.
