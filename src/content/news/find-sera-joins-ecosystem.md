Findsera is now archived and available as a reusable <a class="cta" href="https://github.com/aolaru/findsera.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener">open-source Astro starter</a>. The retained site is noindex and is not operated as a shopping, affiliate, advertising, or analytics service.
